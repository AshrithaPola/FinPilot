## 1. When do you charge an overdraft fee?

We charge $15.00 per item when we pay a transaction that overdraws your
available balance by more than $50.00. If you're overdrawn by $50.00 or less
after the transaction, there's no fee. See the Schedule of Fees (2026).

## 2. Is there a limit?

We charge no more than 2 overdraft fees on any business day.

## 3. Can a fee be refunded?

Contact Customer Care within 60 days of the statement that shows the fee.
Each request is reviewed on its own; a refund isn't guaranteed.

## 4. How do I avoid overdrafts?

transfers in online banking.
